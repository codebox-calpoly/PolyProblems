Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CodeBox ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your personal data and your right to privacy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us through the use of the app:

Personal & Account Data

  • Email Address: Required for account creation, authentication, and unique user identification via Supabase.
  • User Name: Associated with your account for display within the community.

User Content & Media

  • Photos: Required for the "Report Flow." Photos you upload are stored in our database and shared publicly within the app.
  • Location Data: Optional. If provided, geographical coordinates are stored to give context to your reports.

Usage Data

  • Engagement: We track interactions such as voting on posts to manage community features and calculate totals.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Authentication: To securely manage your account and profile.
  • Core Services: To facilitate the reporting and voting system that drives the CodeBox experience.
  • Public Feedback: To display user-generated reports and photos to other members of the community.
  • Relational Mapping: To connect your user ID to your specific contributions (reports/votes) in our database.

3. Data Storage & Third-Parties

Your data is processed and stored using industry-standard service providers:

  • Supabase: Used for database management, storage of media, and user authentication.
  • Vercel: Used for hosting our application services and API.

These providers have access to your information only to perform specific tasks on our behalf and are obligated to protect your data.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Apple and global privacy regulations grant you specific rights regarding your data:

  • Account Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated personal data directly within the app's Settings menu.
  • Data Access: You may request a copy of the data we have stored about you.
  • Location Opt-out: You can revoke location permissions at any time through your device system settings.

Upon deletion, your personal information will be permanently removed from our production databases within 30 days.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this policy, please reach out to us via our GitHub repository or project support channels.
