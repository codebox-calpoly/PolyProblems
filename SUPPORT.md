## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**What should I do if the app crashes?**

Please ensure you are on the latest version of the app. If the problem persists, email us with your device model and a brief description of what you were doing when it crashed.

**How do I report a physical campus issue?**

Use the reporting feature in the app to snap a photo and tag the location. For immediate campus emergencies, please contact Cal Poly Facilities or UPD directly.

**My report says "Pending Approval." What does that mean?**

To ensure the feed remains helpful and safe for all Mustangs, every submission is reviewed by our team. Once an admin verifies that the report meets our community guidelines, it will be approved and visible on the public feed.

**The issue I reported has been fixed. What next?**

If your reported issue has been fixed, go to that report from your **Profile** page and mark the report as **Resolved**. This helps the community know which campus concerns have been addressed.

## 📜 Community Guidelines
To keep Poly Problems a helpful space for all Mustangs, please ensure your reports are:
* **Constructive:** Focus on clear issues that can be resolved.
* **Respectful:** Avoid posting personal information or disparaging content.
* **Relevant:** Ensure reports are related to the Cal Poly campus and student life.
