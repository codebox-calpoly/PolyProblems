Poly Problems is a community-powered app that lets Cal Poly students and staff report campus issues — from broken HVAC units and faulty door locks to dining outages and Wi-Fi dead zones — directly to the departments that can fix them.

Every report gets upvoted by the community, so the most impactful issues rise to the top. No more lost emails. No more wondering if anyone noticed. Just fast, transparent action on the things that matter most to campus life.

How it works
1
Hit the report button
Open Poly Problems and tap the report button from the main feed. It's always one tap away — no navigating through menus. Select the category that best fits your issue to get it to the right team immediately.
2
Fill out the details
Describe the issue, pin the location on campus, and attach photos if you have them. The more detail you add, the faster facilities, safety, dining, or IT staff can diagnose and resolve the problem.
3
Confirm and submit
Review your report and submit. It's instantly routed to the right department and posted to the community feed where other students can upvote it — pushing high-priority issues to the front of the line.
